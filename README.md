# BeamNG Traffic Tuner

Take control of AI traffic in BeamNG.drive!

BeamNG Traffic Tuner is an in-game UI app that lets you customize AI traffic behaviour during Freeroam sessions.

### Features
- **Driving Style:** Adjust AI aggression from careful to assertive.
- **Speed Limit:** Set a maximum traffic speed or remove the cap.
- **Traffic Density:** Control the number of active AI vehicles.
- **Vehicle Awareness:** Adjust how AI drivers react to surrounding vehicles.
- **Presets:** Choose Calm, Natural, Lively, or Smooth.
- **Overtaking:** Enable AI overtaking and passing behaviour.
- **Passing Flashes:** AI vehicles can flash headlights before overtaking.
- **Emergency Yielding:** Traffic reacts to active emergency vehicles.
- **Crash Response:** Involved drivers can pull over with warning lights.
- **Road Rage:** Customize the percentage of traffic drivers with a braver personality.

### Installation
1. Open Freeroam.
2. Go to **UI Edit → Add App → Traffic Tuner**.
3. Add the app and start AI traffic.
4. Customize your traffic directly from the panel.

### Notes
- Most settings apply automatically.
- Your settings are saved.
- Uses BeamNG.drive's native AI and traffic systems.
- Does not replace or modify original game files.
